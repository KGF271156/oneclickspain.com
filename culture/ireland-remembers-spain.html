Why the Irish Came to Spain: The Irish Side of the Story

By the OneClick Spain Editorial Team · 2 min read

In Part Two we followed the Irish families who became part of Spain. In Ireland, the story begins in a different place: the Spanish landing at Kinsale in 1601, the Flight of the Earls and the soldiers, students and priests who became known as the Wild Geese.

It also takes in the Armada wrecks on the Irish coast, and the legends that grew up around them, and, in the 1930s, the Irishmen who travelled to Spain to fight in the Civil War on opposite sides. Our sister site OneClick Ireland tells all of this from the Irish point of view.
