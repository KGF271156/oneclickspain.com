Do You Have an Irish or Spanish Surname With a Story?

OneClick Ireland and OneClick Spain

Our two-part series on Ireland and Spain has brought up one question again and again: how many families on each side still carry a name, a story or a connection that crosses the sea?

We would like to hear from you. If you have an Irish surname with a Spanish branch, a Spanish surname that looks Irish, or any family story that connects the two countries, send it to us.

What to send

The surname, and any other spellings you know; the place the family came from; what you know about the connection (who moved, when and why); and any documents, photographs or family papers you are happy to share. Even a few lines is enough.

How we will use it

With your permission we will feature the best stories on OneClick Ireland and OneClick Spain, and where we can we will point to the archives that might help you find more. We will only publish names and details that you agree to share, and you can ask us to remove them at any time.
